Place

Stand at the place and press the blue button, or tap the map / drag the red target to the exact building.

Photos are shrunk to about 1280 px automatically. On a phone you can take one with the camera.

Connect to your site

Places and photos are saved straight into your GitHub repository, so every visitor sees them after the site redeploys (about a minute). Only someone with your token can change anything.

Advanced: GitHub address (proxy)

The token stays only in this browser. See DEPLOY.md → “Create the admin token”.

“Try without GitHub” loads the places already in this site; your edits stay in this browser and you can download places.json to publish by hand.

Admin sign in

Only the admin account can add, change or delete places. Visitors can only read.
